01 · Pressure
Profile the shot, not just pull it.
Up to 12 bar, fully profiled. Flat 9 bar for classics, lever-style decay for sweetness, or draw your own curve. The pump follows it. Every shot, every location.
FLAT · LEVER · CUSTOM · 0.5 BAR RESOLUTION
02 · Temperature
Roast-matched, and it holds.
PID-controlled temperature. Set 88.0 °C for a dark roast or 96.0 °C for a bright light one, and it stays there. The 49 mm basket is the new standard: sized for the machine, open for your gear. No proprietary nonsense.
85.0 TO 99.0 °C · ±0.5 °C · 49 MM STANDARD

03 · Control
Simple on the machine. Everything in the app.
The on-device UI stays deliberately simple: the essentials, readable at a glance. Full control — profiles, temperatures, shot logs, updates — lives in the app. And if you want everything on the machine, the modular port on top takes an optional advanced UI module.

04 · Power
One machine. Any battery you own.
Modular by design. The Founders Edition ships with a native B-mount and a DC power port. Snap on an adapter and it runs off V-mount, A-mount, or the power-tool packs already in your bag.
NATIVE
B-mount + DC port
Built into the Founders Edition. Plug in the DC port at home or clip on a B-mount pack. No adapter needed.
ADAPTER
V-mount and A-mount
The film-industry standards. Snap on an adapter and your existing cine batteries run the machine all day.
ADAPTER
Power-tool packs
The batteries already in your bag. One adapter, espresso at the trailhead or on the job site.
